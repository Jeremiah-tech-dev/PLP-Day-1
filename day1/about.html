About QuickNotes

QuickNotes helps you capture ideas, reminders, and tasks before they slip away. Keep your notes in one simple place so you can focus on what matters next.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Type an idea or task in the New note field.
  3. Select Add Note to add it to your list.
  4. Review your notes whenever you need a reminder.

Features

  • Quickly capture ideas and reminders.
  • See your notes together in one list.
  • Keep track of how many notes you have.
